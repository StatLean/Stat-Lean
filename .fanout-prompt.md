## Notes on specific targets

**`ConvexMinimizers`** (start here — cheapest, and `Equivariance` consumes it):
- `lintegral_convex_even_min_at_zero`: for `X` symmetric about `0` and `ρ` convex even, `∫⁻ ρ(X − v)` is minimized at `v = 0`. Route: `ρ(x−v) + ρ(x+v) ≥ 2ρ(x)` by convexity+evenness, then symmetry of the law turns the average of the two shifted integrals into the `v = 0` one. Stated for an **arbitrary** measure — no finiteness needed.
