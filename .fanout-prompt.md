## Notes on specific targets

- **`HalmosSavageCriterion`** (`isSufficient_iff_rnDeriv_comp`): the mixture `ν` is supplied as hypothesis data, so this is the criterion itself, not the existence. (⇒) the θ-free conditional determinations give a `T`-measurable version of `dP θ/dν` — this is where `CondExpWithDensity` earns its keep. (⇐) if each `dP θ/dν` factors through `T`, the common conditional determination is `κA t := (dν restricted to A)/dν` evaluated through `T`; verify the defining identity by `setLIntegral` change of variables.
- **`Factorization`** (Fisher–Neyman, both directions): with densities `p θ` w.r.t. σ-finite `μ`, `IsSufficient P T ↔ ∃ g h, IsFactorizedDensity p μ T g h`. Route through `HalmosSavageCriterion` with `ν` from `exists_equivalent_countable_mixture`: take `h := dν/dμ` and `g θ := dP θ/dν` factored through `T`. Mind that `IsFactorizedDensity` is the **a.e.** form.
- **`Minimal`** (Thm 6.12 + Cor 6.13): the likelihood-ratio vector `x ↦ (p i x / p 0 x)ᵢ` is minimal sufficient for a finite family with a.e.-positive densities. Sufficiency by factorization; minimality because any sufficient `U` makes the ratios `U`-measurable (Cor 6.13's criterion), so the LR vector factors through `U` by Doob–Dynkin (`Measurable.exists_eq_measurable_comp`). `hfin` (a.e. finiteness of the ℝ≥0∞ densities) is a `LEAN-ONLY` hypothesis — use it.
- **`RegularConditional`** dominated version: build the kernel from the mixture `ν`'s regular conditional distribution given `T` (`condDistrib` on standard-Borel `𝓧`), then show it works for every `P θ` because `dP θ/dν` factors through `T` — that is exactly `condExp_withDensity_comap_ae_eq`.
