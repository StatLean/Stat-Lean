## Order of attack

1. **`NoncentralChiSquared` (8)** — everything downstream needs it. `noncentralChiSquared k l` is the pushforward of `multivariateGaussian (√l • e₀) 1` under `‖·‖²`. Targets: the probability-measure instance; direction invariance (any mean of the same norm gives the same law — orthogonal invariance of `multivariateGaussian`, and the repo has `AsymptoticStatistics.ForMathlib.MultivariateGaussianSmul`); `noncentralChiSquared_zero = StatLean.MultipleTesting.chiSquared k`; tail monotonicity in `l` (the repo's Anderson lemma is the intended route); and the large-`k` normalization limits, which are displays (16.28)–(16.30) of the source.
2. **`DKWUniform`** — the n-uniform empirical-process tail. Constants are deliberately **non-sharp**: `4·exp(−d²/8)`, chosen because with mean bound `E[√n Dₙ] ≤ 2` a McDiarmid step only covers `d ≥ 2` and the sharper `4e^{−d²/2}` leaves a gap near `d ≈ 1.66`. Both are implied by Massart's sharp form. Keep the stated constants; `ConcentrationInequalities`' McDiarmid is available.
3. **`KSConsistency` / `KSLocalPower`** — the KS test is calibrated by the **DKW threshold** `√(8·log(4/α))`, not by the Kolmogorov limit law. That is a deliberate design decision: it needs **no continuity of `F₀`** and makes the local-power bound exact rather than slack. The single coupling numeral is the `8` in that threshold, tied to the `1/8` in `DKWUniform` — if you change one you must change the other, and say so.
4. **`ChiSquaredMultinomial`** — Pearson's statistic ⇒ `chiSquared (k−1)` under the simple null; the repo's `MultivariateCLT` and `CramerWold` are available, as is the now-closed `ForMathlib.LindebergCLT` if a triangular-array form helps. Then the local-alternative noncentral limit.
5. **`ChiSquaredMaximin` / `SmoothTest`** — assemble on top of 1 and 4.
