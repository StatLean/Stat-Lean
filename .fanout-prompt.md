## The obstruction, and the way through
`exists_measurable_argmin` (~line 179) currently demands `(hcont : ∀ ω, Continuous (f ω))` for `f : Ω → ℝ → ℝ≥0∞`. The consumers cannot supply it: their objective is
```
fObj z w = ∫⁻ x, ENNReal.ofReal (ρ (δ₀ x - w)) ∂(orbitCondKernel … z)
```
and for an unbounded convex loss `ρ` this genuinely **jumps to `∞`** at the boundary of its finiteness domain, so it is not continuous on all of `ℝ`. (The retained-debt notes in both consumers say exactly this.)

**Key mathematical fact to exploit:** a **convex** function on `ℝ` is automatically **continuous on the interior of its finiteness domain**, and **coercivity forces every minimizer to lie in that interior** (the objective is `∞` outside a bounded set only in the coercive direction; on the finiteness domain it is finite and convex). So convexity + coercivity already give you continuity exactly where it matters. Two acceptable routes — pick whichever compiles cleanly:

**Route A (preferred): add `exists_measurable_argmin_of_convex`** to the brick file — same conclusion, but replacing `hcont` by **lower semicontinuity** (`∀ ω, LowerSemicontinuous (f ω)`), which the consumers get for free from **Fatou** (`lintegral` is lower semicontinuous under `liminf`: `lintegral_liminf_le` / `Filter.Eventually` + `ENNReal.ofReal` continuity of `ρ` in `w`). Internally, recover what the existing proof needs by restricting to the finiteness domain `D ω = {w | f ω w < ⊤}`: on `interior D ω` convexity gives continuity (`ConvexOn.continuousOn_interior`-style), coercivity confines the argmin to a compact subset of it, and the existing rational-infimum measurability trick then applies on that set. If `⨅ v : ℝ, f ω v = ⊤` (degenerate fibre) return any constant — the conclusion still holds.
