## Context that matters

The score is **derived from the densities** and is junk-safe: `score M θ x = deriv (fun t => M.density t x) θ / M.density θ x`, with real division so `x/0 = 0`. `fisherInfo M μ θ = ∫ score² · density dμ`.

Two hypotheses on `cramer_rao` are load-bearing and must be **used**, not circumvented:
- `hsupp : HasCommonSupport M` — **without it the theorem is false.** The junk-safe score vanishes off the support while `∫ δ · ∂p` still integrates there, so the covariance identity breaks.
- `hmeas : AEStronglyMeasurable (score M θ) μ` — unavoidable, since integrability of `score² · p` cannot recover the score's sign.

The file also records that `hscore_int` is *redundant* given `hI : 0 < fisherInfo` (a non-integrable integrand makes the Bochner integral `0`); it is kept because the signature is frozen. Do not remove it.

## Notes on specific targets

- `Basic`: `integral_score_eq_zero` is differentiation under the integral applied to `∫ p_θ = 1`; `fisherInfo_eq_variance_score` follows since the score has mean zero. `fisherInfo_reparam` is the chain rule. `fisherInfo_eq_asymptoticStatistics` is the bridge to the vdV-area bilinear `fisherInformation` evaluated at the derived score — read that file's signature and match it exactly.
- `CramerRao`: the whole content is Cauchy–Schwarz on `cov(δ, score)`. `g' = cov(δ, score)` comes from the frozen `hdiff`/`hswap` pair; then `real_inner_mul_inner_self_le` or `inner_mul_le_norm_mul_norm` gives `g'² ≤ var(δ) · I(θ)`; divide by `hI`. `cramer_rao_of_deriv` is the same argument without assuming unbiasedness.
- `Additivity`: `fisherInfo_prod` — the score of a product density is the sum of the coordinate scores, and the cross term vanishes because each score has mean zero under its own factor (independence). `fisherInfo_pi` is the `Measure.pi` induction; `Finset.sum` of variances.
- `DensityRegularity`: the point of this file is that the regularity lives on the **family**, not the estimator. `diff_under_integral_of_density_regular` is dominated convergence on the difference quotients under the frozen envelope hypothesis; `integral_score_eq_zero_of_density_regular` is the real Cor 5.17; `cramer_rao_of_density_regular` then discharges the estimator-side conditions of `cramer_rao` and **concludes a conjunction** (`HasDerivAt … ∧ bound`) — the derivative is a consequence here, not an input. `cramer_rao_iid` substitutes `n · I(θ)` via `fisherInfo_pi`.
