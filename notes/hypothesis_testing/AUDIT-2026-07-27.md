## Open statements (3) — status

1. **`edgeworth_studentized_uniform`** — under active work. Closed so far: (S1) the bivariate
   Edgeworth expansion, (S2) the exact reduction of the studentized root to a bivariate mean,
   (M1)(b) the mixed-characteristic-function expansion, (M3)(i) direction-uniformity of the
   multivariate Cramér condition, and the deterministic half of (M2). Remaining: three purely
   probabilistic residues (one-factor Berry–Esseen bookkeeping; the truncation tail bound with
   its anti-concentration companion; the Cramér condition for the parabola-carried bivariate law
   with Hall's conditioning).
2. **`cornishFisher_studentized_quantile`** — a corollary of 1 by implicit-function inversion;
   adds no independent analytic content.
3. **`bentkus_berry_esseen_convex`** — quotes the *sharp* Bentkus (2003) rate
   `400 k^{1/4} β/√n`. **Zero consumers repository-wide**; the weakened statement at the
   elementary rate `C_k (β/√n)^{1/4}` is proved as `berryEsseen_convex_elementary`.

## Finding that needs a human decision — citation convention

The campaign plan binds this area to the NonparametricStatistics convention: *never name the
textbook in `.lean` files*; book numbering lives only in `notes/`; files carry a
"Bibliographic comments" paragraph citing primary literature. In fact the textbook is named in
**~203 places in HypothesisTesting** and **83 in PointEstimation** (e.g. "E.L. Lehmann and
J.P. Romano, *Testing Statistical Hypotheses*, 4th ed.", "Lehmann–Romano Lemma 18.3.1").
NonparametricStatistics is compliant (2 mentions). Note the earlier PointEstimation audit
passed this because it checked that every result *has* a citation, not that no textbook is
*named* — and PointEstimation is already merged to `main`. Either both areas get rewritten to
the convention or the current style is ratified; not decided here.
